# Apple adapter attribution

The Apple client packages Mineradio's original public/ frontend without claiming
authorship of its visual design. Mineradio and this derivative retain GPL-3.0-only
and the upstream notices in LICENSE and NOTICE.md.

The native Netease protocol, endpoint payloads and response mappings are adapted
from the installed NeteaseCloudMusicApi 4.32.0 util/crypto.js, util/request.js,
module/*.js and Mineradio server.js. The bundled application does not include a
Node runtime, a hosted account proxy or proprietary music-provider binaries.
